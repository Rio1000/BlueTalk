ConnectBlue Support

Offline messaging over Bluetooth — no internet, no account.

Getting started

  • Open ConnectBlue on two phones that are near each other.
  • Tap New chat — each phone advertises and scans automatically.
  • Tap the other device when it appears; once connected, start messaging.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Wi-Fi or cell service?
No. ConnectBlue works entirely over Bluetooth, with no internet connection.

Why can't I see the other person?
Make sure both people have the app open and Bluetooth enabled, and that you're within Bluetooth range. Range depends on your surroundings.

Where are my messages stored?
Only on your device. There is no server, and nothing is uploaded.

Does it work with Android?
Yes — ConnectBlue talks to the Android version over Bluetooth Low Energy.
